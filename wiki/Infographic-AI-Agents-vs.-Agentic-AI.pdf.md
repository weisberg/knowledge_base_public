What are they?

What is it?

Modular systems for narrow, task-specific
automation, driven by LLMs (Large Language

Models) and LIMs (Large Image Models).

Enhance LLMs with external tool use, function
calling, and sequential reasoning.

Core Characteristics (Fig. 4)

Autonomy: Minimal human intervention
post-deployment.

Task-Specificity: Optimized for narrow,
well-defined tasks.

Reactivity: Responds to changes in its
environment and inputs.

Key Applications (Fig. 9)

Customer Support & Internal Enterprise Search

A paradigmatic shift to systems of multiple,
specialized AI agents that collaborate,

communicate, and dynamically allocate sub-
tasks to achieve complex, high-level objectives.

Marked by multi-agent collaboration, dynamic

task decomposition, persistent memory, and
orchestrated autonomy.

Multi-Agent Collaboration: Specialized
