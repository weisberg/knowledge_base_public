# Vanguard Nevada 529 Plan Research Findings

## Plan Overview

**Official Name:** The Vanguard 529 College Savings Plan  
**State Sponsor:** Nevada (Nevada Trust administered by the office of the Nevada State Treasurer)  
**Investment Manager:** The Vanguard Group, Inc.  
**Distributor:** Vanguard Marketing Corporation  
**Program Manager:** Ascensus Broker Dealer Services, LLC (day-to-day operations)  

## Key Features

### Cost Structure
- **Average Expense Ratio:** 0.14%
- **Industry Average:** 0.48%
- **Positioning:** Among the lowest costs in the industry

### Assets Under Management
- **Total AUM:** Approximately $185 billion as of December 31, 2024
- **Position:** One of the largest 529 plan money managers

### Investment Options
1. **Target Enrollment Portfolios (TEPs):** Hands-off, age-based portfolios that automatically adjust
2. **Individual Portfolios:** 20+ portfolios for hands-on investors

### Contribution Limits
- **Lifetime Maximum:** $500,000 per beneficiary
- **Annual Gift Tax Limit:** $19,000 per year ($38,000 if married filing jointly) in 2025
- **Minimum Initial Investment:** 
  - $3,000 (general)
  - $1,000 (Nevada residents)
  - $50 (online enrollment through employer)

### Tax Benefits
- Tax-deferred growth
- Tax-free withdrawals for qualified education expenses
- State tax deductions vary by state (Nevada has no state income tax)

### Flexibility
- Can be used for K-12, college, trade school, apprenticeships, study abroad
- Student loan repayment (up to certain limits)
- Beneficiary can be changed to qualified family members
- Roth IRA rollover option: up to $35,000 lifetime (SECURE 2.0 Act)

### Qualified Expenses
- Tuition and fees
- Room and board
- Books and supplies
- Computers and equipment
- Student loan repayment
- K-12 tuition

## Availability
- Available to any U.S. citizen or resident alien age 18 or over
- Open to residents of all states (not just Nevada)

## Marketing Positioning
- Emphasizes low costs
- Highlights expert management and scale ($185B AUM)
- Focuses on flexibility (spending, beneficiary changes, Roth IRA rollovers)
- Two investment approaches (hands-off vs. hands-on)

## Website Features
- State tax benefits comparison tool
- 529 plan comparison tool
- State tax deduction calculator
- College savings calculator
- Educational resources and articles

4. **My529** (Utah): $27.3 billion

### Top 5 Program Managers by Total Assets
1. **Ascensus**: $147.6 billion
2. **American Funds**: $104.1 billion
3. **TIAA**: $64.9 billion
4. **Fidelity**: $51.7 billion
5. **State of Utah**: $27.3 billion

### Market Overview
- **Total 529 Market Size (Q2 2025):** $568 billion across 17.3 million accounts
- **529 Savings Plans:** $543 billion across 16.4 million accounts
- **Prepaid Tuition Plans:** $25 billion across 900,000 accounts
- **Year-over-Year Growth:** 11.8% (from $508B in Q2 2024)
- **Net Inflows Q2 2025:** $4 billion (up from $3.6B in Q2 2024)

### Vanguard's Position
- **Nevada Vanguard 529 AUM:** $40.7 billion (3rd largest plan)
- **New York 529 Direct (also managed by Vanguard):** $47.6 billion (2nd largest)
- **Combined Vanguard-managed 529 AUM:** ~$88.3 billion
- **Vanguard's Total 529 Management:** Approximately $185 billion (includes multiple state plans)
- **Market Share:** Vanguard is one of the largest 529 plan money managers

### Competitive Analysis
- **CollegeAmerica** (American Funds) is the largest single plan at $104.1B
- **Vanguard Nevada 529** is the 3rd largest at $40.7B
- **Gap to #1:** Nevada plan is 61% smaller than CollegeAmerica
- **Gap to #2:** Nevada plan is 14.5% smaller than NY 529 Direct
- **Advantage over #4:** Nevada plan is 49% larger than Utah's my529

## Morningstar Ratings (2025)

### Gold-Rated Plans (Top Tier)
1. Alaska - T. Rowe Price College Savings Plan
2. Illinois - Bright Start Direct-Sold College Savings Plan
3. Massachusetts - U.Fund College Investing Plan (Fidelity)
4. Pennsylvania - Pennsylvania 529 Investment Plan
5. Utah - my529 (Vanguard/DFA funds)

**Note:** Nevada's Vanguard 529 plan did NOT receive a Gold rating from Morningstar in 2025.

### Rating Methodology
- **Process Pillar:** 50% weight (investment quality, rigor, resilience)
- **People Pillar:** 25% weight (experienced management)
- **Parent Pillar:** 25% weight (state oversight)
- **Fee Evaluation:** Modified z-scores vs. peer group median

### Key Findings
- 59 plans reviewed (representing 90%+ of industry assets)
- 31 plans earned Bronze or better (Gold, Silver, Bronze)
- 5 Gold, 13 Silver, 13 Bronze ratings
- Utah's my529 (Gold) uses Vanguard and DFA funds
- Massachusetts U.Fund (Gold) managed by Fidelity
